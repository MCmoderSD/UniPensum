Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

The UniPensum app does not collect, send or share any data. It has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. Your timetable stays on your phone.

This policy applies to the Android app UniPensum (package name de.mcmodersd.unipensum) and to this website. It explains which data is processed, why, and which rights you have, in the sense of Art. 13 of the GDPR.

01 Controller

The controller is Seraphin Berger (MCmoderSD). You can reach me at business@mcmodersd.de. Full contact details are in the Imprint.

02 Data on your device

UniPensum stores what you enter, on your phone: semesters, courses, events and sessions, lecturers (name, e-mail address and phone number, if you add them), links such as a Moodle or meeting link, notes, and your settings. This data lives in the private storage of the app. The app cannot send it anywhere, because it has no internet access, and I do not receive or see it.

03 Permissions

The app asks only for what its reminders need:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to show a reminder before an event.
  • Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM, and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM up to Android 12L) to show the reminder on time.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) to set the reminders again after the phone restarted.

It does not ask for internet access, location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone or any other permission.

04 Backups

Backup file. In the settings you can export all your data to one file. You choose where it is saved with the file picker of Android, and the app gets access to that one file only. If you set a password, the file is encrypted (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your password by PBKDF2). I cannot recover a forgotten password. Where you keep the file, for example in a cloud storage app, is your choice, and the policy of that provider applies.

Android backup. The app allows the backup and the device transfer of Android for its database and its settings. If backups are switched on in the settings of your phone, Android may copy this data to your Google account or to a new phone, as it does for most apps. This is done by Android and Google under their terms. I have no access to it. You can switch it off in the system settings of your phone.

05 Other apps and links

When you tap a web link, an e-mail address or a phone number, the app hands it to your browser, your mail app or your phone app. From then on the policy of that app applies. UniPensum itself makes no network connection.

06 This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, a company of Microsoft. When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical data of the request (such as date, time, page and browser) in its server logs, see the privacy statement of GitHub. I do not receive these logs.

The website has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no embedded content, and it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other hosts. Its links lead to other sites, such as GitHub and the imprint at mcmodersd.de, where their own policies apply.

07 Legal basis

I do not process personal data of app users, so there is no legal basis to name for the app. If you write to me, I process your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my interest in answering inquiries). The server logs of the website are the responsibility of GitHub as the host.

08 Who else sees this data

Nobody sees the data of the app, and I do not sell or share it. Your own choices can involve others: your cloud storage if you save a backup there, Google or your phone maker if you use the backup of Android, and the browser or mail app you open a link with. For the website it is GitHub as the host, as described above. For e-mails it is your mail provider and mine.

09 Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app, clear the storage of the app in the settings of Android, or uninstall the app. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them. Backups of Android follow the rules of Google or your phone maker. Because I hold no data about app users, there is nothing for me to delete. E-mails to me are kept only as long as needed to answer them, and then deleted.

10 Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction, deletion and restriction of the personal data I process about you, you can object to the processing and ask for portability, and you can complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Write to business@mcmodersd.de. The app gives me no data about you, so in most cases I will have nothing to hand out. The data in the app you manage yourself, in the app.

11 Security

The data is kept in the private storage of the app, which other apps cannot read, and the app has no network connection. Backup files can be protected with a password.

12 Children

UniPensum is made for students and is not aimed at children. It does not collect data from anyone, children included.

13 Changes

If the app or this website changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example through a new permission, I update this policy before the change reaches you. The date at the top is the date of the last change. The history of the file is public in the repository.
